### Hi, I'm Patrick

I study computer science at Texas A&M (class of '28) and mostly build full-stack and AI stuff, usually for problems I run into myself. More at [patricksemler.dev](https://patricksemler.dev).

Right now I'm a research software engineer with the AISFS group at A&M, working on a [beef supply-chain simulator](https://github.com/patricksemler/beef-chain-simulator). It models 30 million head of cattle over 10 years in well under a second, using USDA data that refreshes on its own.

Over the summer I worked on a veterinary diagnosis tool in the same group (shrinking a random-forest model enough to run on Vercel, plus a RAG pipeline on pgvector). I was also a software engineer intern at [Finch](https://applyfinch.com), where I rebuilt onboarding and worked on job matching.

**Things I've built**

- [LeetMind](https://leetmind.patricksemler.dev) ([code](https://github.com/patricksemler/LeetMind)): coding practice that figures out which algorithm topics you're weak at and generates problems for them. A second model solves each generated problem, and it only gets published if both solutions agree on every test.
- [LiftLedger](https://liftledger.patricksemler.dev) ([code](https://github.com/patricksemler/LiftLedger)): my workout and nutrition dashboard. It pulls in Hevy and Apple Health, and I log meals by texting photos to a Telegram bot.
- [AudioTag](https://github.com/patricksemler/AudioTag): a bulk audio tag editor for Windows and macOS that works like a spreadsheet.
- [Nanochain](https://github.com/patricksemler/Nanochain): a small proof-of-work blockchain in C++17.

I mostly write TypeScript and Python, with some C++. Day to day that's React, FastAPI, Hono, and Postgres/Supabase, deployed on Vercel or Cloudflare.
